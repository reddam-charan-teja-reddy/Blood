Problem Statement:

Critical minutes wasted on WhatsApp,
most of these requests reaches non interested or non intented donors 
When someone needs blood urgently, families post in WhatsApp groups, 
get flooded with unverified responses, and waste hours coordinating manually. 
There's no way to verify donors, no structured handoff, and no follow-up loop.

Solution:

Unified platform for emergency blood requests and donations to cut down response time and increase accountability.
Instant match → verified contact → donation
A platform that lets any user post an emergency request in under 2 minutes, 
instantly surfaces compatible verified donors nearby, and improved visibility
and exchanges contacts only after a consent-based OTP flow — creating an accountable, 
abuse-resistant pipeline.


Tech Stack:
- Reactjs.
- Express - Nodejs.
- MongoDB, Mongoose, GeoSpatial queries.
- Authentication: JWT, OTP verification(twillo), Bcrypt, RBAC(Middleware).
- css
- State management: Zustand


Core Features:
1. Signup: Individual and Organizations.
    - Organisation Request submit details of organization and admin will verify and approve the organization.
    - users can either verify during the sign up page or later in the profile page.
2. Login: Secure login with OTP verification or password, 
    - user can browse the app without verification but cannot post requests or contact donors or will to donate.
    - Organization login with only email and password.

3.  Home page:
    1. Navbar
        - Logo
        - Requests /Donor Home Page
        - Find Donors /Patients
        - Profile Page /Edit Profile and verification status, documents
    2. Request Blood Page:
        - Form to submit emergency blood request with details like blood group, location, urgency, and contact information.
        - Option to upload medical documents for verification.
    3. Find Donors Page for Patients:
        - Shows a list of verified donors and eligible donors based on blood group and location.
        - Each card hides donor details and there will be a button to request contact or
          chat which will send a notification to the Donor.
        - Card shows blood type, distance, and last donation date.
        - shows accepted or willing donors for the request with the donor details shown and can chat or contact.
        - Critical Request will send notification to all eligible donors without the need to request contact.
        - If a donor accepts the request, the card will show the donor details and contact information.
        - The patient can mark the accepted donor as rejected or reserved or completed after donation and the status will be updated for both parties.
        - the request/:id page will also show accepted donors and their details and contact information.
        - It shows the status and after completion of donation, 
          the patient can mark it as completed which will update the status for both parties 
          and stored in the profile as donation history.
    4. Donor Home Page:
        - Shows a list of nearby requests matching the donor's blood type and location.
        - Each card hides sensitive patient details and there will be a button to accept or chat which will send a notification to the patient.
            - If accepted by the donor, the card will show the patient details and contact information
            - This acceptance process is skipped for critical patients.
            - If both parties accept, the card will show the contact details of both the patient and donor.
            - The donot can mark the accepted patient as reserved which temporarily blocks the request for other donors and the status will be updated for both parties.
            - The donor can mark the request as completed after donation and patient can select that it's completed, which will update the status for both parties.
            - The donation history will be stored in the profile for both parties.
                - It will be shown as verified only if they upload the supporting documents and verified by the AI or a program or admin or Org if the patient or donor is an organization.
        - Card shows blood type, distance, and urgency level.
        - Search and filter options to find specific requests based on blood type, location, and urgency.
        - shows accepted or willing requests for the donor with the patient details shown and can chat or contact.
    5. Profile Page:
        - Displays user information, verification status, and donation/request history.
        - Option to edit profile details and upload verification documents.
        - For organizations, it will show the organization details and the admin can verify the requests
          posted by the organization and also verify the donors or patients if they are an organization.
        - Users can change their availability status for donors and patients.
        - Users status will be updated after donation or request completion and stored in the profile as history.
        - This will make the donor ineligible for donation for a certain period of time based on the blood type.
    6. Notifications:
        - Real-time notifications for new requests, donor acceptances, and updates on request status.
    7. Chat System:
        - In-app chat feature for patients and donors to communicate securely without 
          sharing personal contact information until
    8. Admin Dashboard:
        - Admin can manage users, verify organizations, and oversee the platform's operations.
        - Admin can also verify the requests and donations 
          if the patient or donor is an organization or if the documents are uploaded for verification.
        - Admin can also verify flagged requests or accounts for abuse or spam.
    9. Organization Dashboard:
        - Organizations can manage their profile, verify their members, 
          and oversee the requests posted by their members.
        - Organizations can also verify the donors or patients if they are an organization.
